- [AI’s medical promises](https://garymarcus.substack.com/p/breaking-eli-lilly-ceo-becomes-latest): Gary Marcus collects criticism of decade-scale cure predictions, including Eli Lilly CEO Dave Ricks’s view that incomplete biological knowledge and randomized human evidence constrain progress.

- [Choosing Zig over C](https://www.mtsoukalos.eu/2026/11/why-zig-over-c-for-systems-programming/) makes a practical case for nearby cleanup, explicit errors and checked slices, while acknowledging remaining memory hazards, build-mode differences and language instability.

- [No AI Slop](https://www.slopmanifesto.com/), written by AIE Code Summit participants, argues that creators should own the intent and consequences of AI-assisted work, optimize for readers’ attention and improve the system when mistakes recur.
- [Haskell’s technical vision](https://discourse.haskell.org/t/a-technical-vision-for-the-haskell-ecosystem/15078): the Foundation’s technical leadership committee prioritizes faster iteration, better debugging and onboarding, supply-chain security and longer compatibility, inviting openly reviewed funding proposals.
