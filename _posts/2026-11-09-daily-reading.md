- [Just-in-time bitmap text](https://linebender.org/blog/jit-bitmap-text/): Raph Levien generates ARM code for proportional text just before each scanline, trading cheap regeneration for scarce RAM; expanding the monochrome result to colour costs more than generating it.
- [Moving from Claude Code to Pi](https://caleb.software/posts/claude-to-pi.html): Caleb describes trading a growing feature set for a smaller, extensible harness, citing unstable workflows and inconsistent editor integration.
- [Goldman’s coding-agent restrictions](https://www.thestateofai.com/news/goldman-sachs-claude-code-traders-bankers): State of AI, citing Bloomberg, reports that most bankers and traders lost access while engineers and strats retained it after a productivity and risk review.
- [NVIDIA’s independent inquiry](https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/statement-from-nvidia-board-of-directors): The board says it will investigate reports of a foreign-intelligence effort targeting Jensen Huang, while stating it has no reason to believe national security or company secrets were compromised.
- [Llama-modchip](https://github.com/synchopate/llama-modchip) reports fitting a heavily quantized 284-billion-parameter DeepSeek model into 64 GB of RAM, combining CPU kernel changes and speculative decoding; its speed and quality figures are project measurements.
- [Person of Interest](https://krthr.co/person-of-interest/): Wilson Tovar revisits the series through memories of early online communities and sees its surveillance premise as newly relevant.
- [Writing as a thinking tool](http://muratbuffalo.blogspot.com/2026/11/writing-as-thinking-tool.html): Murat Demirbas argues that drafting exposes gaps in understanding and creates new connections, recommending freewriting followed by ruthless editing.
- [Outlook and missing DMARC records](https://mailroute.net/blog/outlook-rejecting-email-without-dmarc): MailRoute reports intermittent rejections affecting small senders as well as bulk mail, distinguishing its observed behaviour from Microsoft’s published high-volume rules.
- [KetchUp](https://ketchup3d.github.io/app/) offers open-source 3D modelling for woodworking, with exact dimensions, local project files and SketchUp geometry import in a desktop browser or native app.
- [RAM Altman](https://respawnfirst.com/linux-script-emails-sam-altman-unused-ram-ram-altman/): Sikandar Mahmood describes a satirical Linux memory-monitoring script; email delivery is deliberately unconfigured by default, and a dry run previews its messages.
- [Dux Modular Shell](https://duxlabs.dev/) brings file management, monitoring, calendars and matching application themes to several Wayland compositors through a modular desktop interface.
- [Tesla after Straubel](https://cleantechnica.com/2026/11/07/tesla-hasnt-had-a-successful-new-product-launch-since-jb-straubel-stepped-down-as-cto/): CleanTechnica questions the company’s new-product record since its former CTO stepped down, while acknowledging that his influence and reasons for leaving remain uncertain.
- [OpenAI’s latest funding](https://247wallst.com/investing/2026/11/07/openai-just-raised-11-billion-at-a-1-25-trillion-valuation-below-the-1-4-trillion-it-first-sought/): Omor Ibne Ehsan reports an $11 billion first close at a $1.25 trillion pre-money valuation, distinguishing wired funds from a possible follow-on still unclosed.
- [A shared format for agent memory](https://www.lorekit.io/blog/open-agent-memory-format): Mads Thines proposes agreement on scope, identity, authorship and expiry, separating portable records from the storage and ranking systems that use them.
- [Fighting AI task fatigue](https://sergeykuhne.com/blog/fighting-ai-brain-fry): Sergey Kühne describes a Git-backed work queue and one decision inbox that let him close one task’s context before taking the next.
- [Petered](https://aworkinglibrary.com/writing/petered) considers a darker extension of the Peter Principle: career pressure can reward risky or unethical work rather than simply stopping an ineffective leader’s promotion.
- [iSponsorBlockTV](https://github.com/dmunozv04/isponsorblocktv) applies community-marked sponsor and intro skips to YouTube television apps, and can mute ads or press Skip when it becomes available.
- [Choosing to code without LLMs](https://www.polso.info/why-i-will-not-be-using-llms-coding): Kristian Polso weighs occasional help against his experience of fatigue, weak output and maintenance costs, and values writing code as part of his own problem-solving.
- [Chimera Vocal Synth](https://chimera-vocal-synth.vercel.app/) models airflow through a throat, mouth and nose, exposing tongue, jaw, pitch and breath controls alongside vocal-tract visualisations.
- [ZapFast](https://github.com/crmne/zapfast) implements a native WhatsApp companion in Rust without a browser engine, with encrypted local history; the project reports about 200 MB idle memory in its Linux test and notes the account risks of an unofficial client.
- [PacketHound](https://packethound.app/) builds outbound approval prompts on Windows Firewall rules, showing which application is connecting; its design blocks the first attempt, so an allowed application must retry.
- [Deno’s permission boundaries](https://goldstein.lol/posts/deno-security/): Max Goldstein argues that runtime API checks and terminal prompts cannot replace an operating-system sandbox, especially when permitted subprocesses inherit configurable environments.
- [The web is more than enough](https://ftrain.com/aboard-the-web-is-more-than-enough): Paul Ford argues that AI-assisted access to mature browser APIs lets individuals build rich applications with far less effort, while making software’s economic value less certain.
- Duaa Eldeib investigates a newborn’s fatal bleeding, tracing missed vitamin K prevention, delayed emergency treatment and the Texas Medical Board’s findings against the treating physician. [Investigation](https://www.propublica.org/article/vitamin-k-shot-texas-lonestar-emergency-room-nicole-malouf)
- The UK government announces up to £15 million for book gifting to reach 300,000 more preschool children in England, with programme design details still to come. [Announcement](https://www.gov.uk/government/news/government-announces-up-to-15-million-to-bring-the-joy-of-reading-to-300000-more-young-children)
- Alex Pliutau argues that AI coding shifts engineers toward maintaining code they understand less well, raising concerns about lost learning, codebase growth and inflated delivery expectations. [Essay](https://packagemain.tech/p/the-future-of-software-engineering)
- Israel Laguan’s cleaned Codex dataset contains 437 tool-use runs and 314 delegation examples from one developer; its card warns that related train and test sessions prevent an independent generalization test. [Dataset](https://huggingface.co/datasets/israellaguan/codex-tool-use-trajectories)
- Tencent’s DSpine draft model generates candidate tokens in parallel for Qwen3.8-27B; its card reports faster decoding in concurrent agent replays, rather than demonstrating faster completion of live agent tasks. [Model card](https://huggingface.co/tencent/Qwen3.8-27B-DSpine-b16)
