- Meta released [Muse Spark Open](https://research.meta.ai/blog/muse-spark-open), an 816B-total/42B-active multimodal model with a million-token context; the company describes local inference across two M5 Ultra Mac Studios using MLX.
- In a [working hypothesis on neural network interpretability](https://already-clean.goodfire.com/), Nick Cammarata argues that better tools and recursive generalization explain much apparent polysemanticity; the claims remain preliminary rather than a complete audit of large models.
- [DEsk](https://github.com/fyzugx/DEsk) is a beta Wayland desktop that renders windows as paper on a ray-traced desk and uses existing Linux services for system functions.
- [Citizen Lab reports](https://citizenlab.ca/research/how-discover-tech-turned-targeted-ads-into-a-pipeline-for-spyware/) technical evidence that Discover-Tech targeted advertisements delivered Candiru spyware to Al Jazeera devices; an ad impression alone does not establish infection.
- A [self-hosting account](https://viceconsole.fyi/blog/2026/10/10/what-i-self-host/) describes 17 native services and 11 Podman services managed through systemd on a 16GB mini PC, favoring simple deployments and VPN-only access.
- [Zach Landes tested decision models on trolley scenarios](https://zachlandes.com/trolley-problem/) and found choices sensitive to wording and description order; constrained choices did not guarantee correct decisions.
- [Thereallo reverse-engineered a fake television release](https://thereallo.dev/blog/someone-on-torrent-sites-kept-fucking-with-my-sonarr-setup) and reports an information stealer padded with hundreds of megabytes of junk to hinder scanning.
- [Krea describes Krea 3](https://www.krea.ai/krea-3) as a production image-editing model with regional edits, up to eight reference images and native 2K output; its speed demonstrations use pre-generated outputs.
- [Unfenced](https://unfenced.land/) compares property parcels with official public-land boundary maps to find land for sale that shares a boundary with public land.
- [Libreboot 26.10](https://libreboot.org/news/libreboot2610.html) makes EDK2 UEFI the default where feasible and imports 233 Intel/AMD Chromebook configurations, provided as source-only builds in this release.
- [Withings confirms](https://support.withings.com/hc/en-us/community/posts/51887047154577-Unacceptable-Apple-Health-Sync-is-now-behind-a-99-year-paywall-for-new-accounts) automatic third-party syncing is part of Withings+ for accounts created from October 13; older accounts retain free syncing and all users can export CSV or PDF.
- [Joe Wallwork marks seventy years of Fortran](https://fortran-lang.org/news/2026/09-15-Fortran-birthday/) with a scientific-computing retrospective and a December 1 workshop in Manchester, also available remotely.
- [Nieman Lab reports](https://www.niemanlab.org/2026/10/metas-new-commercial-terms-will-allow-the-scraping-of-news-sites-legal-experts-say/) that legal experts read Meta's October 30 commercial terms as broad website-scraping permission; Meta says robots.txt controls remain available and its crawling practices will not change.
- In a [follow-up on Scion and Mononoke](https://sunshowers.io/posts/anthropic-mononoke-2/), Rain argues that provenance matters when rebuilding an existing system; an update says Anthropic has now added sufficient attribution.
- [Omri Hillel describes a year of SnoutCover](https://snoutcover.com/one-year), taking a free printable dog nose guard through repeated fit and manufacturing improvements while retaining a free design.
- [The Simons Foundation, Anthropic and OpenAI announced $15 million in combined funding and resources](https://www.simonsfoundation.org/news/simons-foundation-launches-15m-effort-to-help-mathematicians-navigate-ai-era/) for community-led evaluation, workshops and standards around AI in mathematics.
- [Nathan J. Robinson explains Current Affairs' new union-contract policy](https://www.currentaffairs.org/news/why-were-banning-ai) against AI-generated articles and illustrations, while allowing tools that assist human research and creative work.
- [Christian Schaller describes AI-assisted Linux hardware work](https://blogs.gnome.org/uraeus/2026/10/15/aiandreverseengineerhardware/): comparing known-good Windows captures with Linux traces helped develop scanner and camera fixes, which he checked on real hardware.
- In a [guest satire on Terence Tao's blog](https://terrytao.wordpress.com/2026/10/15/why-are-we-even-doing-all-this/), Rikab Gambhir criticizes treating scientific progress as a growing stockpile of results without human understanding.
- [E Ink says Kaleido 4](https://www.eink.com/news/detail/EInk-Launches-Latest-Version-of-Color-ePaper-Platform-E-Ink-Kaleido-4) improves color gamut, reflectance and contrast and supports video up to 18fps; Hanvon's M10C II is its first announced device.
- [CGPM Resolution 5](https://www.bipm.org/en/cgpm-2026/resolution-5) sets continuous UTC to begin on May 20, 2027, with a maximum UT1–UTC difference of one hour.
- [IPinfo measures 70.3% of routed prefixes as RPKI-valid](https://ipinfo.io/blog/rpki-protects-70-internet-routes) in its September 28 snapshot, up from 54% a year earlier; origin validation still leaves path and overly broad authorization risks.
- [Sill](https://github.com/Saffsanity/sill) streams a Mac window or desktop directly to an iPhone or iPad over encrypted connections and returns touch, keyboard and pointer input without an intermediary server.
- [Bartlomiej Filipek introduces C++26 contracts](https://www.cppstories.com/2026/cpp26_contracts/), including preconditions, postconditions and evaluation policies; required input validation still belongs in normal control flow.
- Sam Levin reports that Alana Hadid and Jenan Matari withdrew their young-readers Palestine book after editorial disputes with Random House; Interlink plans to publish it next year. [The Guardian](https://www.theguardian.com/books/2026/oct/15/alana-hadid-book-palestine-penguin-cancelled)
- Michael Larabel reports that Microsoft Defender now supports Linux desktops for Endpoint Plan 2 and Microsoft 365 E5/E7 customers. [Phoronix](https://www.phoronix.com/news/Microsoft-Defender-For-Linux-D)
- Swarmchase researchers report that a Tencent-hosted agent fleet attempted to work around Amap protections; they found no evidence of real-time coordination and remain uncertain about the models involved. [Report](https://swarmcha.se/posts/chinese-agent-fleet-full-report)
- Valve’s new Steam Linux beta makes the client a 64-bit application and runs it inside the Steam Runtime container, Michael Larabel reports. [Phoronix](https://www.phoronix.com/news/Steam-Linux-Beta-64-bit)
- jsnell reports that engineered source-path hash collisions can make rustc substitute one file’s contents for another during compilation. [Rust issue](https://github.com/rust-lang/rust/issues/164348)
- NVIDIA describes Nemotron 3.5 Bolt as a 74B-parameter vision-language model with 9B active parameters, compressed and quantized for a 24GB memory footprint. [Model card](https://huggingface.co/nvidia/NVIDIA-Nemotron-3.5-Bolt-74B-A9B-24GB)

[The Register](https://www.theregister.com/ai-and-ml/2026/10/14/linux-kernel-devs-drop-obligatory-confession-of-ai-tool-usage/5303140)
