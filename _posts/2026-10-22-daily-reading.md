- [Simon Edwardsson's WorldTUI](https://simedw.com/2026/10/21/world-model-terminal/) learns terminal screen updates in a 360-million-parameter model; his experiments distinguish plausible output from reliable memory and use parallel drafts with sequential verification to reduce latency.

- Véronique Hyland examines how shrinking book criticism, blockbuster publishing and readers’ wish to join the conversation concentrate attention on a few titles. [ELLE](https://www.elle.com/culture/books/a73764195/reading-monoculture-book-trends/)
