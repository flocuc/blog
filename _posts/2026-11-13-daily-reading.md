- [ZigZag](https://github.com/simpsoka/zigzag) turns SVG artwork into embroidery stitches, exports common machine formats and communicates directly with Brother’s Skitch over Bluetooth; Kath Korevec presents it as an alternative to the machine’s subscription app.
- [Nvidia’s leadership transition](https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/nvidia-announces-ceo-transition): the board announces Jensen Huang’s immediate resignation, appoints CFO Colette Kress as interim CEO and names Harvey Jones its first chair while searching for a permanent successor.
- [Anthropic’s IPO page](https://ipo.anthropic.com/us/) reports $17 billion in quarterly revenue alongside a 3% operating loss margin, distinguishing that result from its positive adjusted margin and a much larger net loss driven by non-cash valuation charges.
- [Gemini 4 Hydrogen](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/gemini-models/introducing-gemini-4-hydrogen/): Google positions its faster model as an execution partner for deeper reasoning models, with adjustable thinking levels and an Antigravity orchestrator that switches models within a task.
- [Rust 1.100](https://blog.rust-lang.org/2026/11/12/Rust-1.100.0/) stabilizes the never type, core allocator APIs and closure-based drop guards, while Cargo adds unused-dependency warnings and optional minimum publication ages for dependency updates.
- [Correctness and complexity](https://pron.github.io/posts/correctness-and-complexity): Ron Pressler argues that general verification faces fundamental limits, while useful progress comes from restricted properties, practical tools and empirical study of the failures programmers actually produce.
- [Apache HTTP/2 race condition](https://www.wiz.io/blog/cve-2026-119618-rce-apache-http-server-vulnerability): Wiz describes a heap-corruption flaw affecting versions 2.4.55–2.4.70, fixed in 2.4.71; it reports no public exploit or observed exploitation at publication.
- [Smolmaxxing](https://github.com/hyperionaidev/smolmaxxing/) reports running language models on an old iPad and a small TV box through chip-specific kernels, reduced memory use and mixture-of-experts weights paged from flash.
- [A distant dwarf-planet candidate](https://www.smithsonianmag.com/smart-news/astronomers-found-a-mysterious-object-orbiting-far-beyond-pluto-it-could-be-a-new-dwarf-planet-hidden-in-our-solar-system-180989857/): Carlyn Kranking reports that archival images establish 2026 ED138’s orbit, but its uncertain size still prevents a firm dwarf-planet classification.
- [Zen 6’s design tradeoffs](https://chipsandcheese.com/p/talking-zen-6-with-mike-clark-at): AMD’s Mike Clark tells George Cozma how lower clock targets permit larger queues in dense cores, while front-end efficiency, cache bandwidth and quick recovery from branch mistakes remain priorities.
- [Model-weight hardware fingerprints](https://unemploymentlabs.com/checkpoint-forensics): Will DePue reports initialization patterns matching Blackwell-class hardware in six Chinese models; the method does not establish chip location, access arrangements or an export-control violation, and initialization need not identify all training hardware.
- [Jane Street’s interview changes](https://blog.janestreet.com/software-engineering-interviews-are-changing-a-little/): Matthew Saffer describes campus trials emphasizing design, code assessment and some LLM use, while most rounds remain conventional coding interviews without model assistance.
- [Who pays for data-center power](https://news.cornell.edu/stories/2026/11/data-centers-need-power-lower-income-rural-residents-pay-price): Cornell researchers report that historical cost-sharing rules shift much of new grid infrastructure spending onto households, and propose demand-based charges and coordinated reductions during peak hours.
- [PostgreSQL’s changing-type flaw](https://mehmetince.net/part-3-6-how-i-ended-up-turning-a-22-year-old-postgresql-type-confusion-primitive-into-rce-cve-2026-6460/): Mehmet Ince traces how cached composite values can outlive changes to their field types, leading to memory corruption; he describes coordinated fixes and credits the wider group of researchers behind the umbrella issue.
- [Apple’s Foundation Models framework](https://developer.apple.com/foundation-models-framework/) presents a shared Swift interface for local and server models, with image inputs, guided generation, tool calls and profiles that switch models and tools within one session.
- [LibreAGI](https://libreagi.com/) proposes preserving open-weight models through a members-only tracker, source-verified files and a signed public catalogue designed to survive the hosting site.
- [A Linux pinball engine](https://www.getlazarus.org/forums/topic/17/): its developer describes a work-in-progress FOSS simulator with force-sensitive sound, live table editing and script-controlled cameras, with physics improvements still underway.
- [An eight-minute radio pulse](https://www.curtin.edu.au/news/media-release/researchers-discover-rare-star-system-that-emits-radio-signals-every-eight-minutes/): Sam McSweeney’s team links a long-period transient to a white dwarf with a Sun-like companion, locating the emission near the white dwarf through its orbital motion.
- [Synth developers and App Review](https://synthanatomy.com/2026/11/ai-is-turning-apples-app-review-process-into-a-nightmare-for-developers.html): Synth Anatomy reports lengthy delays and disputed rejections; developers suspect automated reviews, but the article does not establish Apple’s use of AI as their cause.
- [ExcalidrawZ](https://github.com/chocoford/ExcalidrawZ) wraps the drawing canvas in native Apple document management, version history and synchronization, with AI proposal previews and per-file controls over model visibility.
- Daniela Tijerina profiles Dua Lipa’s Service95 book club, whose selections and author interviews aim to bring challenging books to new readers. [Vanity Fair](https://www.vanityfair.com/story/dua-lipa-book-club)
- Sarah Perez reports that Hugging Face’s Clément Delangue reaffirmed support for open-source AI after Jensen Huang’s departure; Nvidia’s proposed acquisition remains scheduled to close in the first half of 2027. [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/12/hugging-face-ceo-vows-to-keep-fighting-for-open-source-ai-after-jensen-huangs-exit/)
- Microsoft’s incident updates describe an overwhelmed secure-connection process disrupting Exchange Online and dependent services, with recovery efforts spanning restarts, traffic limits and configuration reversions. [Service-health reports mirrored by Penn](https://mailservices.isc.upenn.edu/computing/email/penno365/alerts/ms-incidents.html?q=EX1550543)
- [OpenCode’s font specimen](https://opencode.ai/font) builds six letters from a 4 × 7 modular grid, exposes their outlines and spacing, and gives each downloaded font a character-substitution map.
