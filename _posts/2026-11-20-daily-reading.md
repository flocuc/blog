- [SQLite-backed blob storage](https://zerodha.tech/blog/high-performance-distributed-blob-storage/): Sarat Chandra explains Zerodha's sharded SQLite store for UI preferences, reporting roughly 2 ms production p99 latency while accepting weaker durability and static cluster placement.
- [Anthropic's Nasdaq debut](https://www.reuters.com/business/finance/anthropics-historic-debut-put-ais-promise-peril-wall-st-test-2026-11-19/): Reuters reports an $81 billion IPO and a 17% first-day gain, while distinguishing a roughly $2 trillion fully diluted valuation from outstanding-share value.
- [A pancreatic cancer safety trial](https://www.newscientist.com/article/2528235-pancreatic-cancer-halted-by-virus-injection-in-three-patients/): Alice Klein reports stable disease in three patients after low-dose engineered-virus injections; the uncontrolled preliminary trial does not establish efficacy.
- [Apple's encryption order](https://macanorak.com/uk-government-forced-to-admit-it-issued-apple-a-secret-encryption-order/): Alexander Cohen reports that the UK tribunal required acknowledgment of a Technical Capability Notice to Apple; it did not decide the order's legality or disclose its technical demands.
- [A developer's safeguards critique](https://ounapuu.ee/posts/2026/11/19/unsafeguards/): Techtipsy says Claude blocked authorized security work on their own service, and argues that exemption requirements burden defenders who need to verify fixes.
- [Claude and black-hole physics](https://www.anthropic.com/research/claude-black-hole-physics): Anthropic reports a weak-coupling phase-transition calculation and an optimal bound for a simplified modular bootstrap, describing independent checks and Lean verification of the latter result.
- [Ruby2Shell](https://verialabs.com/ruby2shell/): Veria Labs describes a legacy-encoding case-conversion overflow reachable through default Rails configurations and lists fixed Ruby releases 3.3.14, 3.4.13 and 4.0.10.
- [Millions of tiny web servers](https://kapteko.com/15-million-webservers/): Artem Yarulin's experiment moves TCP handling into a custom user-space runtime to reduce per-server memory, comparing smoltcp with DPDK and AF_XDP.
- [An earlier Java heap-corruption report](https://github.com/openjdk/jdk/pull/32781): An OpenJDK pull request describes how malformed short Base64 tails can produce an invalid output mask in an x86 AVX-512 decoding path.
- [Lastbook](https://lastbook.org/): An open-source offline preparedness app bundles local AI, maps and instructional videos; its medical information is explicitly not clinically reviewed.
- [Storage-price forecasts](https://www.drivevault.io/blog/when-will-hard-drive-prices-come-down.html): DriveVault expects NAND supply to ease SSD prices before hard drives, citing analyst forecasts and manufacturers' long-term capacity contracts; the dates remain forecasts.
- [PHP 8.6](https://www.php.net/releases/8.6/en.php): The release adds partial function application, typed durations, scalable polling and structured stream errors, alongside stricter session defaults and compatibility changes.
- [RAM compression with zram](https://curiousbits.nl/blog/do-not-buy-ram-compress/): CuriousBits demonstrates compressing 5.7 GB of browser memory into 1.7 GB of RAM, trading CPU work and latency for capacity.
- [Foldable browser-made zines](https://krthr.co/paper-folds-and-whats-new-in-fanzines-app/): Wilson Tovar's editor arranges eight pages for a single folded A4 sheet, with local photo handling, spread previews and print-safe margins.
- [Why AI wants to be local](https://thedataexchange.media/pete-warden-moonshine/): Pete Warden argues that local models offer control over cost, privacy and upgrades, and that low latency and visible feedback matter for usable voice interfaces.
- [Paid advertising as a margin squeeze](https://globalcommercegraph.com/writing/the-ninety-eight-percent-slot-machine): Global Commerce Graph argues that competitive ad auctions transfer advertisers' margins to platforms and pitches durable commercial relationships as an alternative.
- [Cornhole News](https://cornholenews.news/): A Hacker News-style site collects Midwest reporting in a ranked list with votes, discussion links and optional political filtering.
- [Cooling versus heating demand](https://maccus.fr/en/cooling-overtaking-heating/): Marc Winer explains the IEA's cooling milestone as useful energy services rather than fuel consumed, and highlights cooling's disproportionate contribution to peak electricity demand.
- [Communication through gravity](https://www.science.org/doi/10.1126/sciadv.aee0802): Andrew Groszek and colleagues transmit a message through a brick wall over roughly 0.7 m at one bit per minute by moving an elevator counterweight; active jamming remains possible.
- [Models Engineering Index](https://git-aarvin.github.io/mei/): The index combines published engineering benchmarks and places Gemini 4 Neon first, while warning that uneven evidence, missing error bars and benchmark differences limit comparisons.
- [Chrome's background bandwidth](https://perishablepress.com/chrome-ai-wasting-much-bandwidth/): Jeff Starr reports that disabling Chrome's AI features stopped unexpectedly high background data use on his setup.
- [Frontier Design Benchmark](https://contralabs.com/frontierdesign): Contra Labs and BenchFlow evaluate outputs from real client briefs through blind designer votes and a 35-dimension rubric.
- [DeepMind's union campaign](https://www.gdmunion.co.uk/): UK employee organizers seek statutory union recognition and bargaining over enforceable AI principles, transparency, the right to abstain and environmental commitments.
- [AI work and platform power](https://restofworld.org/2026/ai-jobs-skills-gig-work/): Julián Posada argues that highly qualified AI contractors face the same unilateral pay changes and disappearing tasks long experienced by global data workers.
- [A European TikTok proposal](https://www.techcentral.ie/germany-and-austria-call-for-european-control-of-tiktok/): German and Austrian officials propose European control of TikTok's regional operations, seeking oversight of user data and recommendation algorithms.
- [Synology's OAuth advisory](https://www.synology.com/en-uk/security/advisory/Synology_SA_26_21): Synology warns of unauthenticated arbitrary file access and denial of service in DSM and BeeStation OS, listing affected versions and deactivating OAuth Service as mitigation.
- [AI value creation versus capture](https://colossus.com/article/ai-will-not-make-you-rich/): Jerry Neumann compares AI with containerization, arguing that competition may shift much of the value toward customers and businesses applying the technology.
- [Foolscap](https://foolscap.works/): David's writing app tracks characters, ideas and unresolved threads while leaving prose to the writer; its private-book mode sends no book content to AI, according to the site.
