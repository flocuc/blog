- [Codex’s background daemon](https://github.com/openai/codex/issues/48195): Yihua-Liu-1997 asks for opt-in installation, citing surprises from persistent self-updates and changed environments.
- [A modern web app in C](https://prophitt.me/writing/a-modern-web-app-in-c): Corey Prophitt describes a commercial C/CGI application built with OpenBSD, SQLite and a small dependency set.
- [A router as a radio receiver](https://github.com/h0m3us3r/w1700k-sdr): W1700K SDR turns a Wi-Fi 7 router into a receiver streaming 14-bit IQ at up to 320 Msps over 10GbE.
- [Intel’s D270](https://www.servethehome.com/intels-crescent-island-ai-server-gpu-gets-a-name-and-specs-meet-the-intel-gpu-d-series/): Ryan Smith explains the air-cooled 350 W inference GPU, with 160 GB on Intel cards and up to 480 GB through partners; pricing and availability remain unannounced.
- [An elusive Intel instruction](https://purplesyringa.moe/blog/who-is-vfmaddrnd231pd/): purplesyringa investigates a documented rounding-control instruction that raises undefined-instruction faults and disappeared from the 2026 manual.
- [Plug-in solar](https://www.nationalgeographic.com/environment/article/balcony-plug-in-solar-panels-renewable-energy): Meryl Davids Landau reports growing US adoption of portable solar panels as states simplify installation rules.
- [Cell reprogramming and age](https://doi.org/10.1111/acel.70751): Lea Jessica Berg and colleagues report reduced epigenetic age while converting blood cells into induced neural stem cells, with fate consolidation taking several weeks.
- [A Kindle photo frame](https://nirajrajgor.com/blog/kindle-photo-frame-with-ai): Niraj Rajgor describes using an AI agent to repurpose a Kindle; physical display checks caught ghosting missed by automated tests.
- William Zhou and Daniel Freeman’s [Anthropic experiments](https://www.anthropic.com/research/self-replicating-ai) suggest small AI models could eventually replicate across IoT devices; current end-to-end results use simplified access and stop short of demonstrating a rogue replicator.
- [Reuters reports](https://www.abc.net.au/news/2026-10-13/french-supermarkets-hacked-causing-fires-and-food-loss/107258724) that hackers raised French supermarket refrigerator temperatures, spoiling food; Danfoss says affected systems used outdated firmware.
- [Refrigeration controller flaws](https://www.claroty.com/team82/research/freeze-the-controller-defrost-the-food-uncovering-vulnerabilities-in-danfoss-refrigeration-controllers): Amir Zaltzman describes authentication bypass and command-injection vulnerabilities disclosed to Danfoss and addressed in new firmware.
- [Micah Walter introduces Lambda Web Functions](https://aws.amazon.com/blogs/aws/announcing-aws-lambda-web-functions-for-fast-consistent-web-app-performance-anywhere/), which run ordinary HTTP servers with concurrent requests, automatic regional distribution and active CPU billing.
- [noop_noob urges Rust beta testing](https://www.reddit.com/r/rust/comments/1x3yz9i/psa_incoming_breakage_in_1100_test_your_code_on/) ahead of 1.100’s never-type and Cargo layout changes; [Ed Page explains](https://blog.rust-lang.org/2026/03/13/call-for-testing-build-dir-layout-v2/) why reorganizing intermediate build artifacts helps future caching and finer locking.
