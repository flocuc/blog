- [Advice for Rust GUIs](https://linebender.org/blog/next-100-guis/): Raph Levien calls for modular interfaces, solid text and accessibility foundations, and better compositor integration rather than treating Rust alone as a performance solution.
- [Lean community verification](https://leanprover-community.github.io/blog/posts/riemann-hypothesis/): The Lean and Mathlib communities certify that Alpöge's proof matches Mathlib's Riemann Hypothesis statement and passes multiple independent proof checkers, while inviting work to explain its ideas.
- [The Iowa lead-azide disposal dilemma](https://www.science.org/content/blog-post/detonating-huge-pile-lead-azide-really): Derek Lowe explains how deteriorated storage narrowed the Army's disposal options and why a controlled blast still poses persistent lead-contamination concerns.
- [A monitor and a slow Ethernet connection](https://blog.tymscar.com/posts/studiodisplayethernet/): Oscar Molnar traces reduced downloads to adapter buffering and USB burst timing through his display hub; a different adapter eliminated the observed packet drops.
- [Japanese automakers standardize components](https://www.caranddriver.com/news/a74490092/toyota-nissan-honda-standardize-parts-lower-costs/): Jack Fitzgerald reports planned common steel criteria, plastics protocols and wiring colors intended to cut costs without changing visible brand identities.
- [LookAtMyQuant](https://github.com/ShinMegamiBoson/lookatmyquant): The project allocates compression by sensitivity and reports several Inkling 2 Mini builds; its 5.4 GB version saves memory while scoring below the original on math and coding tests.
- [An apparent FBI database breach](https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/cvze7de86pk3o): Jaroslav Lukiv reports an investigation into an apparent NCIC breach, citing US reporting; the FBI had not answered the BBC's request for comment.
- [SimpleBench's latest results](https://simple-bench.com/): Its everyday-reasoning leaderboard places GPT 6.2 Astra and Sol below earlier OpenAI models, illustrating uneven benchmark performance; its human comparison rests on nine participants.
- [Leonida's recompilation work](https://github.com/ItsNotRexed/Leonida): The GTA 6 compatibility project reports shader translation progress but explicitly says the game does not yet boot or offer playable content.
- [Zero C#](https://onurgumus.github.io/zero-csharp/index.html): A bflat fork keeps C# syntax and generics while replacing runtime services with explicit arena lifetimes; reflection, async and recoverable exceptions are absent.
- [OpenRouter's provider review](https://openrouter.ai/blog/announcements/data-processing-incidents-on-inference-providers/): OpenRouter says it removed three providers and 125 zero-retention-eligible endpoints after investigations, notified potentially affected customers and is adding ongoing technical checks.
- [PostgreSQL extensions in Go](https://jespino.github.io/gopgext/): Experimental gopgext uses TinyGo and generated C glue to expose Go code inside PostgreSQL, warning that runtime or generated-code bugs can crash the server or corrupt data.
- [Modeling Anthropic's power supply](https://betterdatacenterproject.com/2026/11/16/how-ai-is-powered-a-breakdown-of-byop-and-grid-power/): Better Data Center Project estimates a heavily fossil-fueled 2028 power mix using contracted sites and marginal grid generation, with substantial uncertainty in utilization and delivered capacity.
- [Upload2Shell](https://pwn.ai/upload2shell): Paulos Yibelo describes a Ghostscript font-loader sandbox escape reached through several document-processing workflows, distinguishing confirmed execution from merely traced paths and listing available renderer fixes.
- [PcapAnalyzer](https://github.com/sebaandrada-cell/PcapAnalyzer): A self-hosted beta flags network and capture-quality problems with 27 detectors; optional AI investigation receives selected metadata rather than the full capture by default.
- [Independent RH verification records](https://math.mit.edu/~drew/grh/): Andrew Sutherland documents reproducible builds, statement comparisons and independent kernel checks, explicitly limiting the checked GRH claims to Dirichlet L-functions.
- [Pandex](https://www.pandex.io/): A legal-data service exposes official European legislation and high-court decisions through an API and MCP, with version history, citation links and institution-level coverage gaps.
- [Canary tokens in AI supply chains](https://www.dragos.com/blog/ai-supply-chain-security-canary-tokens): HD Moore reports that seeded prompts exposed provider compromises, reseller access and retention-policy failures, including later use of planted credentials and links.
- [The mythology of conscious AI](https://www.noemamag.com/the-mythology-of-conscious-ai/): Anil Seth distinguishes intelligence from subjective experience and argues that biological embodiment may matter, while acknowledging uncertainty and warning about persuasive appearances of consciousness.
- [The Riemann Handbook](https://github.com/tamnd/riemann-handbook): A project turns Lean declarations into mathematical prose, explicitly warning that its written account is not an independent check of the mathematics.
- [GrapheneOS's RCS prototype](https://twitter.com/GrapheneOS/status/2124158267123233056): GrapheneOS says its proof-of-concept app supports MLS-encrypted messaging compatible with Google Messages and iOS, and will inform RCS integration into its Messaging app.
- [Autonomous-weapons treaty talks](https://www.theguardian.com/technology/2026/nov/21/international-talks-control-ai-autonomous-weapons-collapse): Frances Mao and Patrick Wintour report that consensus-based UN talks failed to agree a binding-treaty mandate and settled for weaker language.
- [Engineering managers and flattening](https://www.manager.dev/newsletter/the-engineering-manager-s-role-is-under-attack-again): Anton Zaides and Suzan Bond argue that wider reporting spans can erode conflict resolution, career support and coordination, obscuring the organizational costs of removing managers.
- [Fast Anything](https://github.com/EL4CTEO/fast-anything) profiles deterministic functions and asks a coding agent to port selected hotspots to Rust; its replay checks cover recorded and derived cases, rather than proving equivalence for every input.
- [Anthropic’s IPO stabilization option](https://www.reuters.com/markets/us/anthropics-ipo-bank-closed-position-meant-stabilize-stock-removing-source-2026-11-20): Reuters’ Echo Wang reports that Morgan Stanley exercised the full greenshoe at the offering price, increasing proceeds while removing a potential source of buying support as shares fell.
