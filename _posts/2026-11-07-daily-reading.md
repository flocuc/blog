- [Libghostty 1.0](https://mitchellh.com/writing/libghostty-1-0): Mitchell Hashimoto releases a stable C ABI for Ghostty’s terminal-emulation library, with compatibility checks and packaging for independent embedders.
- [Cybertruck production](https://www.nbcnews.com/tech/tech-news/tesla-wind-cybertruck-production-years-poor-sales-markets-rcna607817): Reuters reports Tesla plans to wind down Cybertruck production in Texas around mid-2027 to make room for Cybercab output.

- [Confidence-based model routing](https://hightouch.com/blog/llm-judges-identity-resolution): Hightouch’s synthetic identity-resolution tests found that sending uncertain Jev decisions to Opus preserved accuracy while reducing cost, with savings depending on task difficulty.
- [Deus Ex in context](https://www.filfre.net/2026/11/deus-ex/): Jimmy Maher traces the game’s flexible problem-solving to Looking Glass and reads its story against 1990s conspiracy culture.
- [pwn.college](https://pwn.college/): Arizona State University’s team offers free, hands-on cybersecurity education built around computing fundamentals.
- [How volunteers build the internet](https://thereader.mitpress.mit.edu/from-linux-to-wikipedia-how-volunteers-build-the-internet/): Benjamin Mako Hill, Christian Pentzold and Aaron Shaw explain peer production through shared governance, distributed work and a protected commons, while describing barriers to participation.
- [Gemini Omni 1.2 Flash](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/technology/developers-tools/introducing-gemini-omni-1-2-flash/): Google adds native 15-second video generation and conversational editing, and says the release lowers prices while improving reference consistency.
- [Replacing air-traffic-control floppy disks](https://www.reuters.com/world/us/us-faa-says-it-will-phase-out-floppy-disks-air-traffic-control-by-2028-2026-11-05/): David Shepardson reports the FAA plans to replace four legacy information systems at more than 400 facilities by the end of 2028.
- [Kugelblitz](https://apps.metaebene.me/en/kugelblitz): Tim Pritlove’s Mac globe and atlas combines terrain with open geodata and identifies the sources and licences behind each layer.
- [AI research acceleration](https://www.planned-obsolescence.org/p/ai-is-dramatically-accelerating-ai): Ajeya Cotra interprets METR’s public assessment as evidence of substantial AI-driven research acceleration, while stressing that key evidence is redacted and extrapolations remain uncertain.
- [First steps with UEFI](https://kevinboone.me/efitest_index.html): Kevin Boone outlines a series for Linux developers covering compilation, bootable media and file access in bare-metal UEFI applications.
- [Ubuntu’s critical TCP vulnerability](https://ubuntu.com/security/CVE-2026-98432): Canonical lists fixes for some Ubuntu 26.10 kernels while work continues across other releases, and describes packet filtering as a partial mitigation.
- [Limits of autonomous research](https://www.lesswrong.com/posts/DALNiKBjMGMF8grme/arjun-panickssery-s-shortform?commentId=fJtaHHHfx45tD7BSL): Arjun Panickssery reports weak judgment in several AI research projects, alongside stronger results on tightly measured optimization tasks that still benefited from human direction.
- [Firecracker escape research](https://pwn.ai/blog/firecracker-escape): Pwn.ai demonstrates control of a live Firecracker thread using an earlier host-memory primitive; its root-execution test ran without Firecracker’s production jailer.
- [Worldstrike](https://worldstrike.fun/): A multiplayer toy-tank game builds shared, destructible city environments from OpenStreetMap data.
- [Rune](https://github.com/anomalyco/rune): Anomaly’s prerelease Rust UI framework includes headless rendering and element-tree inspection so developers and agents can check native interfaces programmatically.
- [Notifying compromised websites](https://scotthelme.co.uk/we-know-15-579-websites-are-infected-how-do-we-tell-them/): Scott Helme says Report URI has identified malicious JavaScript on 15,579 sites, but finding reliable contacts and getting a response remains a bottleneck.
- [Sodium-ion battery economics](https://www.pv-magazine.com/2026/11/06/sodium-ion-batteries-approach-cost-parity-with-lfp-with-70-gwh-covered-by-supply-agreements/): Pilar Sánchez Molina reports Wood Mackenzie expects cell-level cost parity with LFP before system-level parity, with projected lifetime savings offsetting higher upfront costs.
- [Unleash](https://github.com/Unleash/unleash): The open-source feature-management platform separates code deployment from release decisions through targeted flags, gradual rollouts and kill switches.
- [NVIDIA Kubernetes Engine](https://github.com/dsx-ai-factory/nvke): NVIDIA’s preview management plane provisions independent Kubernetes clusters on owned GPU infrastructure; its maintainers explicitly advise against production use.
- [Corporate oversight plans](https://www.washingtonpost.com/technology/2026/11/06/after-courting-trump-corporate-america-faces-democratic-reckoning/): Pranshu Verma reports Democrats are preparing investigations of Apple, Meta and Musk’s companies.
