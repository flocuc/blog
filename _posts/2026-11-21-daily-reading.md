- [Developers and architecture decisions](https://manidoraisamy.com/developer-to-architect.html): Mani Doraisamy traces a 15-second welcome-screen delay to generated logging code, arguing that developers must set architectural constraints and challenge costly requirements.
- [Two GRH proof claims](https://alpo.ge/remark.pdf): Levent Alpöge announces a Claude-assisted formalization, while [Ravi Vakil's announcement of Alessandro Masullo's separate proposal](https://math216.wordpress.com/2026/11/20/hurried-announcement-alessandro-masullo-grh/) now notes reported problems and says community review must resolve them before acceptance.
- [Inkling 2 Mini](https://thinkingmachines.ai/news/inkling-2-mini/): Thinking Machines releases a 26.4B-parameter looped model with a roughly 20 GB footprint, reporting stronger token-efficient reasoning while noting that additional loops do not improve factual recall.
- [Tao on new formalized mathematics](https://terrytao.wordpress.com/2026/11/20/unforced-euler-singularity-unforced-navier-stokes-singularity-and-the-generalized-riemann-hypothesis/): Terence Tao distinguishes readable Euler work, Navier–Stokes work needing a professional write-up and an enormous Lean-only GRH claim whose mathematical strategy still needs digesting.
- [Models rewriting their weights](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.21885): Jiajun Fan and colleagues report that a 27B model edited its own weights without gradients and improved accuracy on unseen problems by 6.6 points in their experiments.
- [iOS browser engines](https://blog.mozilla.org/en/mozilla/internet-policy/apple-ios-browser-engine-rules/): Mozilla says Apple's alternative-engine conditions remain impractical, including requiring a separate Firefox app and a manual user migration rather than updating the existing app.
- [Coding practice and review](https://cekrem.github.io/posts/if-you-stop-writing-code-you-also-get-worse-at-reviewing-it/): Cekrem argues that reviewing generated code cannot sustain engineering judgment without continued hands-on coding and problem-solving practice.
- [The AI-linked market sell-off](https://www.theguardian.com/business/2026/nov/20/global-stock-markets-slump-anthropic-ipo-ai-sell-off): Lauren Almeida and Graeme Wearden report that Nasdaq erased its 2026 gains as Anthropic fell 9% on its second trading day, amid wider concerns about AI investment returns.
- [Aaronson on the GRH announcement](https://scottaaronson.blog/?p=10344): Scott Aaronson expects the claimed proof to stand but distinguishes formal certificates from the years of explanation and reconstruction mathematicians may still need.
- [Navier–Stokes formalization repository](https://github.com/tristanbuckmaster/navier-stokes/): Alpöge and Buckmaster's repository describes a Lean package but says the numerical certificate and interval computation are absent, and its latest patched package had not been rebuilt when assembled.
- [Nvidia's SEC inquiry](https://www.marketscreener.com/news/us-sec-s-nvidia-probe-is-examining-potential-accounting-issues-sources-say-ce785ed3dd8ff627): Reuters cites two sources saying investigators are examining potential accounting issues, while noting that an inquiry is not evidence of wrongdoing.
- [Slopstrap](https://slopstrap.mikelacher.com/): Mike Lacher's satirical CSS kit packages glowing cards, animated badges, generic copy and fabricated testimonials into a parody of AI-generated websites.
- [NINLIVE](https://ninlive.com/): The fan-run Nine Inch Nails archive adds streaming for live recordings spanning 1988–2026, alongside concert records, photos and tape-source histories.
- [DreamCoder](https://www.masteringemacs.org/article/announcing-dreamcoder): Mickey Petersen introduces a browser-based coding harness with tiled chats and terminals, keyboard navigation and workspace maps showing agent progress and blockers.
- [An experienced engineer's job search](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50583761): A US engineer describes nearly two years without a software role, depleted savings and a search for work beyond software despite continued technical practice.
- [The making of Oxygène](https://jeanmicheljarre.com/aaa/the-making-of-oxygene): Jean-Michel Jarre recalls recording with limited kitchen-studio equipment, while David Kosten explains reconstructing the eight-track material for an immersive mix without losing its original balance.
- [Re: Riemann](https://borretti.me/article/re-riemann): Fernando Borretti criticizes rhetoric around AI-assisted discovery that, in his view, puts the model ahead of human agency and achievement.
- [Backblaze's December privacy notice](https://www.backblaze.com/company/policy/privacy): The policy discloses advertising-related sharing of identifiers, browsing activity, commercial information and geolocation, with opt-outs through privacy choices and recognized browser signals.
- [And yet, it moves](https://signoregalilei.com/2026/11/20/and-yet-it-moves/): An essay responds to the GRH claim with mixed wonder and grief, contrasting discovering a truth with understanding and achieving it ourselves.
- [An FPGA inference experiment](https://ankitg.me/blog/vibecoding-inference-chip/): Ankit Gupta reports using coding agents to raise a working FPGA model from 6.43 to 213 tokens per second, while retaining a bit-exact reference and noting that comparison systems and long-context results differ.
- opnF reports a KVM flaw that let researchers with root inside a guest read an AMD host's memory; exposure requires nested virtualisation, and DigitalOcean says its fleet has been patched. [Report](https://opnf.sh/news/kvm-vulnerability-cve-2026-99312/)
- Carmen Nesbitt reports that Utah added Sarah J. Maas's *Kingdom of Ash* to its statewide public-school removal list, bringing the total to 45 titles. [The Salt Lake Tribune](https://www.sltrib.com/news/education/2026/11/18/utah-school-book-ban-list-reaches/)
- David Howard examines older-driver safety alongside the loss of independence that driving restrictions can bring, including gaps in crash data and alternatives to driving. [Slate](https://slate.com/life/2026/11/driving-risk-population-aging-elder-care-roads.html)
- Angelique Chrisafis reports that French police are investigating rape and assault allegations against Gérard Depardieu and Michel Houellebecq concerning a 2018 film shoot. [The Guardian](https://www.theguardian.com/film/2026/nov/20/gerard-depardieu-michel-houellebecq-investigated-allegations)
- Unsloth publishes smaller GGUF versions of Inkling 2 Mini, reporting a measured trade-off between download size and agreement with its eight-bit reference. [Model card](https://huggingface.co/unsloth/Inkling-2-Mini-GGUF)
