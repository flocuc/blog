- [TryNix’s browser Linux update](https://fzakaria.com/2026/11/23/2035-came-early): Farid Zakaria reports faster startup after switching to a WebAssembly-compiled Linux kernel, with graphics, sound and relay-based networking; his nested Firefox experiment remained slow and required substantial compromises.
- [Whose agent it is](https://michaelharley.net/posts/2026/11/01/llm-agents-work-for-their-parent-companies-not-for-you/): Michael Harley uses a music-discovery workflow to argue that hosted agents remain constrained by their providers’ rules, and expresses concern about future commercial influence on answers.
- [New York’s vehicle-fee restriction](https://www.governor.ny.gov/news/money-your-pockets-governor-hochul-signs-bill-restricting-exploitative-vehicle-subscription): The governor’s office says a new law will bar recurring or post-purchase fees for pre-installed features without ongoing costs, taking effect February 27, 2027.
- [A suspicious Polymarket position](https://deucalionn.vercel.app/polymarket-delit-initie-jensen-huang-nvidia/): deucalionn traces concentrated purchases before public news of Huang’s return, arguing that the timing merits scrutiny while acknowledging that the trader’s identity and access to privileged information remain unknown.
- [Quad9’s withdrawal from France](https://quad9.net/news/blog/quad9-service-deactivation-in-france/): Quad9 says it is refusing French queries rather than implement court-ordered content blocking, acknowledging that its geographic filtering inevitably misclassifies some users.
- [Ternary Bonsai 2.5](https://prismml.com/news/bonsai-2-5-27b): PrismML reports stronger multi-turn agent performance at the same 5.9 GB footprint, narrowing selected gaps to the Qwen base model without improving every benchmark.
- [Tidal’s AI-music policy](https://variety.com/2026/music/news/tidal-bans-ai-generated-music-1236956924/): Corbin Bolies reports that Tidal will begin removing wholly AI-generated tracks December 9, moving beyond its earlier labeling and demonetization policy; the scale estimates come from Tidal’s leadership.
- [Nvidia stock disclosures](https://www.kadoa.com/congress/insights/trump-nvidia): Kadoa organizes reported trades by Trump and members of Congress, emphasizing reporting delays, value ranges and family accounts that prevent firm conclusions about current holdings or who directed trades.
- [Jetty Pro](http://hypercritical.co/2026/11/23/jetty-pro): John Siracusa describes turning a small folder-palette idea into a Dock alternative over months of refinement, with private APIs and extensive permissions needed to reproduce system behavior.
- [Check Point intrusion campaign](https://www.greynoise.io/blog/check-point-security-management-servers-intrusion-campaign): Steve Miller describes credential theft, relay infrastructure and lateral movement affecting at least 22 organizations; GreyNoise treats AI-assisted operation as an assessment and does not attribute the actor to a nation or known group.
- [India’s opposition and Meta](https://korben.info/en/india-opposition-storms-meta-offices-instagram-accounts.html): Vincent Lautier reports protests over blocked Instagram accounts and the opposition’s claim that two were subsequently restored; accusations of government-directed restrictions remain attributed to the party.
- [Replaying the critical path](https://fzakaria.com/2026/11/12/worshipping-at-the-altar-of-brendan-gregg): Farid Zakaria shows how Rewind combines dependency timing and off-CPU analysis with deterministic replay, distinguishing time spent on a path from the savings possible by removing one part.
- [An API customer’s distillation dispute](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50630809): A poster says Anthropic demanded proof that their company was not distilling models, despite their account that it only ran regression benchmarks, raising a privacy problem in demonstrating a negative.
- [Microsoft’s outage response](https://www.reuters.com/business/microsoft-declares-code-red-after-historic-outage-internal-memos-show-2026-11-24/): Jaspreet Singh reports internal plans for faster recovery, traffic partitioning and clearer diagnostics after fragmented incident coordination hampered the November outage; Microsoft’s one-hour target is a goal, not an achieved capability.
- [Tegmark’s criticism of Anthropic](https://novaramedia.com/2026/11/24/anthropic-staff-openly-talk-about-taking-over-the-world-claims-top-mit-physicist/): Clare Hymer reports Max Tegmark’s account of conversations with employees and his objection to alignment without human control; Anthropic had been approached for comment.
- [Songe](https://github.com/sous-labs/songe) proposes learning from local agent feedback during idle periods, separating instruction updates from adapter training and retaining earlier versions; its privacy and improvement claims come from the project.
- [GPT protein-design results](https://openai.com/index/gpt-6-models-demonstrate-intrinsic-protein-design-capabilities/): OpenAI reports laboratory binding from model-designed proteins, while noting that specialized models still supplied sequences or filtered candidates, full structure prediction lagged, and binding alone does not establish a useful or safe medicine.
- [A television made just smart enough](https://qsantos.fr/2026/11/19/making-a-dumb-tv-just-smart-enough/): Quentin Santos connects an infrared remote and microcontroller to a desktop-based media setup, using separate display sessions to avoid window-placement problems while retaining a conventional television.
- [Long-term cardiovascular patterns](https://www.ed.ac.uk/news/heart-attack-and-stroke-risk-detectable-decades-in-advance): Edinburgh researchers report differences in blood pressure and other risk-factor trajectories decades before events across four cohorts; the study did not analyze ethnicity and does not establish a deployed 30-year prediction test.
- [Visibility behind a CDN](https://charlie.fish/posts/2026/11/what-cloudflare-doesnt-tell-you/): Charlie Fish argues that modern browser-facing encryption and IPv6 can conceal weaker origin connections, and calls for clearer administrator alerts and end-to-end connection disclosure.
- [A researcher’s bug-bounty account](https://www.justafewbugs.xyz/): Jesse Edwin Gordon Li-Yates describes disputed Android report closures, delayed rewards and fixes he identified in shipped binaries, distinguishing confirmed comparisons from paths he could not verify; these are his findings and account of the process.
