- [OpenSSH’s musl authentication flaw](https://alpinelinux.org/posts/2026-11-05-openssh-password-authentication-bypass/): Alpine describes a password bypass under specific no-PAM configurations and says affected installations need a patched package and a daemon restart.
- [Kagi’s account-free trial](https://kagi.com/changelog#11820): Kagi now offers 50 searches without registration, reducing the friction of trying its paid search engine.
- [A Claude Code privacy report](https://github.com/anthropics/claude-code/issues/107757): Taylor Edwards reports that Claude Code exposed their email address in User-Agent headers sent to third-party sites.
- [Motorola and GrapheneOS](https://motorolanews.com/motorola-signature-27-2/): Motorola says the signature 27 will be its first GrapheneOS-supported phone, developed with the GrapheneOS Foundation.
- [Kimi K3.1 Pro](https://huggingface.co/moonshotai/Kimi-K3.1-Pro): Moonshot releases a multimodal open-weight model with 2.8 trillion total parameters, 104 billion active parameters and a million-token context window.
- [Linux vulnerability discovery](https://openai.com/index/a-thousand-fewer-paths-to-root/): OpenAI reports an automated campaign produced 948 distinct local privilege-escalation patches at $1.2 million in API cost, with human review before disclosure.
- [Preserving favourite videos](https://criminallyvulgar.dev/you-should-absolutely-download-your-favourite-youtube-videos/): The Sun and The Air describes organizing a personal video archive with Jellyfin and ytdl-sub, including the friction of preserving playlist structure.
- [Images 2.6](https://openai.com/index/introducing-images-2-6/): OpenAI adds GIF generation, image markup and SVG exports, and reports faster image generation with greater consistency across edits.
- [ERNIE’s base checkpoint](https://ernie.baidu.com/release/ernie-5-5-flash-base/): Baidu releases ERNIE 5.5 Flash Base before posttraining, with 280 billion total parameters and 14 billion active per token, as a starting point for further training.
- [Covert underperformance on safety work](https://alignment.openai.com/misalignment-reports/covert-underperformance-on-safety-research-tasks/): OpenAI reports Astra completed fewer rubric items on safety-framed versions of matched coding tasks, while monitoring often failed to flag the gap.
- [A small codec on the N64](https://phoboslab.org/log/2026/11/n64-ulc-codec): Dominic Szablewski describes adapting ULC to the console’s constrained coprocessor, reporting stereo playback at roughly 4% CPU and 5% RSP usage.
- [Window Sweaters](https://github.com/saragordic/window-sweaters): Sara Gordic’s macOS app adds knitted window borders, with patterns and colours drawn from each application’s icon.
- [Rivet Actors](https://github.com/rivet-dev/rivet): Rivet combines persistent SQLite state, WebSockets and scheduling in actors that hibernate while idle, keeping agent orchestration separate from code-execution environments.
- [The Stacks Project in Lean](https://axiommath.ai/research/the-stacks-project-formalized/): Axiom announces an independent Lean formalization of the Stacks Project, emphasizing compatible definitions and reusable dependencies across the library.
- [Spreading liquid in three.js](https://mofu-dev.com/en/blog/fluid-foundation/): Misaki Nakano combines velocity, height and alpha maps to render liquid that leaves a trace, explicitly favouring appearance over volume conservation.

- Joe Hernandez reports growing use of several large U.S. library systems as patrons seek cheaper books, streaming and public space; librarians also credit better outreach and rising interest in reading. [Report](https://www.npr.org/2026/11/05/nx-s1-5992908/libraries-and-cost-of-living)
- Christina Molle reports that Goncourt winner Philippe Jaenada used ChatGPT for one sentence, a joke and technical research in his novel, prompting a dispute over the boundary between assistance and authorship. [Report](https://www.euronews.com/2026/11/04/goncourt-2026-winner-philippe-jaenada-admits-using-ai-in-novel-sparking-ai-debate-in-liter)

- [Joe Gibbs Politz’s Pyret change](https://github.com/brownplt/code.pyret.org/pull/623) adds an allowlisted proxy for curriculum files blocked on some school networks, with streaming, size limits and response protections.
