- [Compromised Claude Code extensions](https://cursor.com/blog/claude-code-extension-security-notice): Cursor identifies malicious marketplace packages 2.1.312 and 2.1.313 for macOS and Windows, removes them and warns that uninstalling alone may leave persistence behind.

- [Opposing an AI bailout](https://progressives.house.gov/2026/10/reps-casar-waters-lead-democratic-resolution-opposing-a-i-bubble-bailout-for-big-tech-commit-to-protecting-american-people-instead): Greg Casar and Maxine Waters introduce a resolution opposing corporate rescues after a possible AI bubble collapse while supporting help for workers and retirees.

- [elect.horse](https://elect.horse/): A district-drawing puzzle demonstrates how packing and splitting rival voters can turn a losing vote share into a winning seat count.

- [Automation and SRE skills](https://newsletter.signoz.io/p/ai-isnt-replacing-sres-its-deskilling): Elizabeth at SigNoz argues that automating routine incidents can erode the practice needed for unfamiliar failures, recommending deliberate hands-on work and active human involvement.

- [Northflank’s security incident](https://status.northflank.com/): Northflank tells customers to assume environment variables were exposed, reports unauthorized use of customer AI keys and enables audit logs for all accounts.

- [Binance’s rapid theft](https://www.wired.com/story/the-dollar63-billion-binance-hack-was-pulled-off-in-just-20-minutes/): Matt Burgess reports that most of the $6.3 billion theft occurred within twenty minutes; tracing firms suspect North Korean involvement, while Binance has not named an attacker.

- [Azure’s cross-tenant connection flaws](https://www.binarysecurity.no/posts/2026/10/one-root-case): Haakon Wik Gulbrandsrud describes five fixed vulnerabilities where shared connector authority and inconsistent input validation enabled access across tenant boundaries.

- [Proton cancels AI re-interviews](https://www.404media.co/protons-ceo-cancels-ai-re-interviews/): Joseph Cox reports that Andy Yen cancelled the interviews and acknowledged communication failures; employees differ over whether the continuing AI push addresses their concerns.

- [Classic Mac OS on a Wii](https://virtuallyfun.com/2026/10/29/my-favourite-powermac-is-a-nintendo-wii/): Rairii describes the firmware patches and drivers that brought Mac OS 7.6 and 9.2.2 to a Wii desktop, with substantial work still needed before release.

- [Tech shares and AI spending](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cvwy72e0n72yo): Rachel Clun reports sharp losses as earnings and spending plans increase investor scrutiny of the returns from AI infrastructure.

- [Tangled’s federated forge](https://tangled.org/blog/forge/): Tangled moves issues and pull requests alongside code as Git objects, using AT Protocol attestations to support portable repositories and independently hosted services.

- [Patrick Stewart’s Picard](https://www.bostonglobe.com/2026/10/29/metro/patrick-stewart-picard-star-trek-the-next-generation/): Jason Laughlin and John Hilliard remember a portrayal of leadership that combined moral conviction, patience and vulnerability.

- [Florilegium](https://github.com/kevinraymond/florilegium): Kevin Raymond generates floral LaTeX margins from parametric curves, growing ornaments into free page space while fixed seeds keep the result reproducible.

- [Bunny’s certificate precaution](https://status.bunny.net/incidents/p6gf1by9kr7m): Bunny removes a node after a provider incident and reissues TLS certificates with new keys, while reporting no evidence that its server was accessed.

- [Nesformer](https://github.com/dbreunig/nesformer): Drew Breunig compiles an NES CPU into Qwen-shaped weights using Qwenputer’s compiler; graphics and input remain in a host program, and execution is far slower than a console.

- [Linode’s security advisory](https://status.linode.com/incidents/nt58zhx3pmgm): Akamai is deploying mitigations for CVE-2026-98432 and advises workload OS updates, while reporting no evidence of exploitation on its compute platform.

- [GitHub service recovery](https://www.githubstatus.com/incidents/pwxsjgnvrk6b): GitHub resolves delays affecting Actions, Pages and Webhooks, with a detailed root-cause analysis still to come.

- [AI work and mental overload](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50301479): An Ask HN author describes fatigue from parallel tasks, generated text, guardrails and frequent decisions despite increased output.

- [Cisco’s refactoring experiment](https://fortune.com/2026/10/30/cisco-president-jeetu-patel-gen-z-27-year-old-unlimited-ai-tokens-rewrite-legacy-code-entry-level-jobs/): Orianna Rosa Royle reports Jeetu Patel’s backing for a small AI-assisted team, with a goal of refactoring most legacy code over two years rather than a completed result.

- [BeOS in Infinite Mac](https://blog.persistent.info/2026/10/infinite-mac-beos.html): Mihai Parparita brings BeOS versions to the browser and explains how a second operating system exposed emulator assumptions that Mac OS had never challenged.

- [Archaeology emerging from old ice](https://www.sciencenorway.no/archaeology-climate-culture/the-oldest-ice-is-disappearing-the-stone-age-is-emerging-from-the-melting-ice/2713921): Ida Irene Bergstrøm reports increasingly ancient hunting artefacts emerging in Norway as melting ice exposes objects preserved for thousands of years.
