- [Mistral Large 4 weights](https://mistral.ai/news/mistral-large-4-oss/): Mistral releases its trillion-parameter multimodal model with three reasoning modes and reported coding gains; commercial model-service providers need a separate licence.

- [LLM voice](https://martinfowler.com/bliki/LLM-Voice.html): Martin Fowler argues that writing develops thought as well as prose, while recognizing accessibility and translation as reasons to accept model assistance.

- [DoorDash’s proposed Instacart acquisition](https://about.doordash.com/en-us/news/doordash-to-acquire-instacart): The companies announce a roughly $17 billion agreement that would combine grocery technology and delivery networks, subject to shareholder and regulatory approval.

- [H.264 is magic](https://sidbala.com/h-264-is-magic/): Sid Bala explains how discarded visual detail, chroma subsampling, motion prediction and entropy coding work together to shrink video.

- [Alphabet’s changing revenue mix](https://www.kadoa.com/companies/insights/google-search-below-half): Kadoa reports that Search and other advertising fell below half of quarterly revenue because the rest of Alphabet grew faster, even as Search revenue rose.

- [Gemini 4 Carbon evaluated](https://artificialanalysis.ai/articles/gemini-4-carbon-new-number-1): Artificial Analysis puts Carbon first on its intelligence index and second on coding agents, while finding that stronger knowledge accuracy comes with more hallucination than Argon.

- [Firewall compromises and identity infrastructure](https://dreamgroup.com/blog/chained-php2shell-exploit-roots-25-servers): Dream reports a chained attack against PAN-OS systems that reached internal directory services; it distinguishes confirmed credential extraction from later attacks whose outcomes remain unknown.

- [Agents in games](https://labs.ycombinator.com/projects/games/index.html): YC and Olam Labs observe agent teams developing hierarchy, bureaucracy and caution in Minecraft, and different communication patterns in competitive Mindustry experiments.

- [If IPv4 were land](https://iptrust.co/blog/if-ipv4-were-land): IP Trust redraws countries by their share of geolocated IPv4 addresses, making the uneven distribution of address space visible.

- [A record quarter for US battery storage](https://electrek.co/2026/08/31/the-us-added-a-record-20-2-gwh-of-battery-storage-in-just-3-months/): Michelle Lewis reports 20.2 GWh installed in the second quarter, led by utility projects, while residential installations declined.

- [Naming disputed waters](https://publicpolicy.googleblog.com/2008/04/how-google-determines-names-for-bodies.html): Google’s 2008 policy explains how local usage and multiple labels were intended to acknowledge competing names without choosing a side.

- [Herdr’s desktop app](https://github.com/herdrdev/herdr-app): Herdr groups terminals and coding agents into workspaces, keeping local or remote sessions running after the window closes.

- [Tinyworldformer](https://github.com/SheepSeb/tinyworldformer): Two small networks generate and play simple games on a Pico 2 W; the project shows how agents can exploit world-model errors and how fresh training data closes those gaps.

- [State honours for two Brno graduates](https://www.vut.cz/en/but/news-f19528/two-but-graduates-received-state-honors-from-the-president-of-the-republic-d378468): Tomáš Mikolov and Záchranka creator Filip Maleňák receive Czech Medals of Merit for contributions to science and technology.

- [Proton’s AI push](https://www.404media.co/manifestos-fear-and-loser-thinking-inside-protons-ai-reckoning/): Joseph Cox reports employee concerns over AI adoption and security review; Proton acknowledges staff anxiety and says it needs to explain its direction better.

- [Pico CSS ends maintenance](https://github.com/picocss/pico/discussions/754): Lucas Larroche makes version 2.1.1 the final release, leaving packages and documentation online and allowing forks under the existing MIT licence.

- [Automatically refusing local-network prompts](https://github.com/johncassil/dontallow): John Cassil’s small Swift utility presses “Don’t Allow” on matching macOS prompts, with pause and allowlist controls for applications that need access.

- [Rack-aware GPU scheduling](https://skypilot.ai/blog/grace-blackwell-needs-a-new-scheduler): SkyPilot explains why NVLink locality, spare nodes and defragmentation matter; the cited capacity model favours sixteen-node slices over full racks when nodes fail.

- [California’s coastal flooding](https://gizmodo.com/el-nino-is-already-flooding-californias-coasts-and-the-years-highest-tides-havent-arrived-2000832574): Matthew Phelan reports unusual October tidal flooding and researchers’ warnings that winter storms coinciding with higher tides could worsen it.

- [A Kogui perspective on AI](https://sonidoselva.com/perspectiva-kogui): Mamo Vangelio Moscote Alberto and Saka Rosa Mamatacán Sauna argue that AI safety depends on human responsibility, ecological limits and forms of knowledge absent from recorded datasets.

- Swati Khandelwal reports that Linux TCP flaw CVE-2026-98432 has uneven distribution patch coverage; Anthropic’s reported remote-root demonstration used an MPTCP listener, while broader exploitability remains uncertain. - Jay Peters reports that Reddit plans to test mandatory login in selected markets outside the US in the coming months; no global rollout date or final logged-out behavior is settled. [Report](https://www.theverge.com/tech/1022571/reddit-login-requirement-q3-2026-earnings)

- Cohere announces an initial Series E close toward a target of up to C$7 billion, outlining plans for customer-controlled AI deployments and an AISLE-based cybersecurity business. [Announcement](https://cohere.com/blog/who-gets-to-build-the-frontier-for-ai)
