- [Blender 5.3](https://www.blender.org/download/releases/5-3/) adds native Gaussian-splat import and rendering, light dispersion, optional project-wide settings and ripple video editing, with current splat-rendering limitations documented.
- [Fragility in AI-written code](https://evanxmerz.com/post/ai-usage-generates-a-fragile-codebase/): Evan X. Merz argues from his development experience that shallow tests, excess abstractions and rushed review can erode maintainability and human understanding.
- [Scion’s kei-truck return](https://www.theautopian.com/scion-is-back-and-its-going-to-sell-a-freaking-kei-truck-in-america/): Leslie Hanna reports a late-2027 fleet pilot for Toyota’s compact KEI-01, limited to low-speed use rather than an ordinary highway-going pickup.
- [Val Town is winding down](https://blog.val.town/winding-down): Steve Krouse announces paused billing, read-only operation from December 10 and service closure on January 10, with open-sourcing planned for February 1.
- [DisplayLink patent claims](https://lwn.net/SubscriberLink/1102549/acbc92e00cee3a3f/): Joe Brockmeier reports that threatened litigation led Mike Lothian to withdraw his Linux driver work; OIN concluded its codec exclusion left the project outside its protection.
- [Glasswing’s validation bottleneck](https://www.vulncheck.com/blog/anthropic-glasswing-november-update): Patrick Garrity argues that reported discoveries far outpace validated fixes, highlighting curl maintainers’ rejection of many scanner reports and the work required to assess them.
- [Modern Web Guidance 1.0](https://developer.chrome.com/blog/modern-web-guidance-v1) combines browser compatibility data, expert-reviewed implementation guides and code scanning to steer coding agents toward native web features and accessible interactions.
- [Ableton Live 13](https://www.ableton.com/en/live/) introduces step-sequencer clips, automation capture and expanded racks, including new multiband and mid-side options for sound design and mixing.

- [Radio signals as motion sensors](https://maurycyz.com/projects/fading/): A selective-fading experiment detects movement in a neighbouring room from a TV broadcast signal and estimates walking speed from interference patterns.
- [.NET 11 runtime changes](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dotnet/core/whats-new/dotnet-11/runtime) raise the x86 hardware baseline and preview runtime-managed async execution, with clearer live stack traces and additional JIT and WebAssembly improvements.
- [The geo-located generation](https://www.theatlantic.com/magazine/2026/12/location-tracking-apps-parental-surveillance/689031/): Sarah Zhang examines how family tracking can ease logistics yet encourage reassurance-seeking, distinguishing developmental concerns from the limited and mixed research evidence.
- [Tiny València](https://tinyvalencia.com/) presents roughly 112 square kilometres of the city as an illustrated isometric map.
