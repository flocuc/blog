- [Intel One Mono](https://github.com/intel/intel-one-mono): Frere-Jones Type developed this coding font with feedback from low-vision and legally blind developers, offering four weights, matching italics and optional programming ligatures.

- [GPT-6.2 usage accounting](https://twitter.com/thsottiaux/status/2125784147889393806): Tibo says OpenAI corrected an unintended launch discount, making the same work consume more plan allowance, with no retrospective charges and a banked reset for affected subscription tiers.

- [Nextcloud Office Desktop](https://nextcloud.com/blog/euro-office-comes-to-your-desktop-with-the-nextcloud-office-desktop-app/): Nextcloud’s Euro-Office desktop release supports local offline editing, installed fonts and digital signatures, with optional real-time collaboration through Nextcloud Hub.

- [AI and self-hosting](https://janlukas.blog/posts/ai-self-hosting): Jan-Lukas describes using coding agents to reduce setup friction and automate his personal infrastructure, while retaining responsibility for understanding changes and maintaining each added service.

- [An AI-cure tracker’s definition](https://diseasescuredbyai.com/): This tracker distinguishes AI-assisted discovery and clinical candidates from demonstrated cures, requiring an AI-selected target and molecule, regulatory approval and durable disease reversal before counting an entry.

- [AMD-specific llama.cpp patches](https://devnull.land/amd-specific-llama-cpp): The author reports higher prefill and generation speeds with a small AMD-focused patch set on one eGPU setup, distinguishing hardware optimizations from shortcuts that change model routing.

- [Minipg](https://progrium.github.io/minipg/): Jeff Lindsay packages PostgreSQL as a single executable with self-contained database directories and automatic server lifecycle management, warning that the young runtime is unsuitable for irreplaceable data.

- [A cost-control interpretation](https://micjm.com/blog/openai-is-in-cost-control-mode/): This analysis reports fewer reasoning and output tokens in GPT-6.2 battle tasks and interprets the change as cost tuning; the measurements do not establish OpenAI’s intent.

- [YouTube Ad Punisher](https://github.com/antonpictures/youtube-punisher): George Anton’s extension detects ads and reloads while preserving playback position, documenting local ad-history logging and a mirror fallback rather than conventional network filtering.

- [Lions’ Unix commentary](https://fosscomics.com/posts/17.%20Lions%27%20Commentary%20on%20Unix/): This illustrated history traces how Unix licensing restrictions drove decades of unofficial photocopying of John Lions’ teaching notes before their authorized 1996 publication.

- [Kamil Khan Mumtaz](https://worksinprogress.co/issue/the-greatest-architect-youve-never-heard-of-is-in-pakistan/): Works in Progress profiles the Pakistani architect’s turn toward local building traditions and the craft partnerships that recovered brickwork, vaulting and ornamental techniques.

- [Warmer datacenter cooling loops](https://www.theregister.com/hpc/2026/11/26/45c-inlets-just-killed-the-chiller-most-people-havent-noticed-yet/5311831): Dan Olds argues that 45°C liquid inlets can make dry cooling practical in more climates, while emphasizing the engineering required and treating energy and water savings as estimates dependent on site conditions.

- [Workspace agents and MCP](https://techstackups.com/articles/google-workspace-ai-agents-mcp/): TechStackups reports that Google’s coming agent-access rules require its MCP gateway, while several Gmail actions remain partner-only, creating a capability gap for independent integrations.

- [Moat membership](https://commandcode.ai/moat): CommandCode markets pooled model purchasing through a paid membership with discounted token prices and no router fee; usage remains billed separately from membership.

- [Data-broker request automation](https://github.com/gary23w/f-ck_data_brokers): This local Python utility prepares deletion letters and tracks replies, while acknowledging that requests disclose identifying details and a broker’s deletion claim is not independent proof.

- [RansomwareHelp indictment](https://krebsonsecurity.com/2026/11/cto-of-ransomwarehelp-indicted-for-wire-fraud/): Brian Krebs reports allegations that a recovery firm secretly bought attacker decryptors while charging clients for purported cryptographic work; the case concerns charges, not a conviction.

- [Gravity from the null cone](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.34924): Venkatesa Chandrasekaran proposes an algebraic quantization of vacuum general relativity and claims finite amplitudes and black-hole entropy results in an unreviewed preprint.

- [IBM Consulting sponsorship changes](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/nri/work/ibm-gives-h-1b-green-card-holders-3-weeks-to-relocate-from-us-or-leave-company-december-31-deadline-set/articleshow/136180168.cms?from=mdr): The Economic Times reports that most consulting roles will lose US immigration sponsorship, with some affected employees offered overseas transfers or severance; the policy does not cover every IBM division.

- [Cogentic’s factorization claim](https://github.com/google-research/google-research/tree/4a7bc8ab0bc770e71a259b7966c500496b99010a/cogentic_research_papers/integer_factorization): Google’s research artifact claims an unconditional randomized subexponential factorization algorithm, with a companion Lean formalization promised; this is not a polynomial-time claim.

- [Real-complexity separations](https://github.com/samueleverett01/bss-permanent-separations): Samuel Everett’s AI-assisted project claims separations in the Blum–Shub–Smale model, explicitly conditional on cited permanent lower bounds and other hypotheses, and notes that the results lack peer review.

- [Company culture through Slack emoji](https://lia.mg/posts/measuring-company-culture-with-slack-emoji/): Liam Galvin’s satirical metric uses custom emoji and participation to mock corporate culture measurement, including the ease with which its own score can be gamed.

- [Kagi Watch](https://kagi.com/watch): Kagi previews a screen-free walkie-talkie for children before smartphone ownership, with availability still forthcoming.

- [Rvoip](https://github.com/eisenzopf/rvoip): This Rust communications library unifies signaling and media behind a shared conversation model, distinguishing beta-qualified SIP functionality from preview browser and voice-AI features.

- [Olumino](https://olumino.app/): This student-built notes and flashcard app combines Markdown study material, Japanese reading support and adaptive spaced-repetition scheduling, with Anki import and export.

- [NormalScript](https://psychoscript.com/): Anomaly’s embeddable JavaScript-subset VM can suspend runs to bytes and resume them with matching builds, while documenting missing runtime APIs and limits that hosts must account for.

- [Qualcomm’s Oryon 3](https://chipsandcheese.com/p/qualcomms-oryon-3-reaches-the-top): Chester Lam finds desktop-class integer performance in Qualcomm’s laptop core, crediting clock speed, a wider frontend and fast caches, while identifying vector-width and software-ecosystem limitations.

- [Learning to Play Vim](http://themouseless.dev/vim/): Matthieu’s manual develops shared Vim and Neovim features through 21 chapters and 170 exercises, prioritizing built-in editing skills over plugin-heavy IDE replication.

- theZackD’s Rust tutorial builds a small drawing app through pixels, shapes, selection and image export, introducing language concepts as each feature needs them. [Build Your Own Paint](https://www.buildyourownpaint.com/)
