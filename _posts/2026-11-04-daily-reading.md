- [AI and legacy security debt](https://spectrum.ieee.org/ai-in-cybersecurity-php2shell-vulnerability): Dina Genkina describes how AI-assisted vulnerability discovery increases pressure on maintainers who must verify and ship safe fixes.
- [Residential proxy exposure](https://layer3intel.com/blog/residential-proxies-white-house-network): Layer3 Intel reports proxy exits from the White House network while distinguishing observed addresses from the unknown devices or people behind them.
- [Stringman](https://neufangled.com/): Neufangled’s open-source cable robot uses four ceiling-mounted motors for room-scale manipulation and supports local operation.
- [HackerTV](https://hackertv.org/): Danny Salzman turns HackerTube’s video catalogue into themed channels with a weekly refresh.
- [AMD’s revenue forecast](https://www.tomshardware.com/pc-components/cpus/amd-expects-to-outsell-intel-in-q4-for-the-first-time-in-history-usd17b-guidance-would-exceed-intels-projections): Anton Shilov reports AMD’s $17 billion fourth-quarter guidance would exceed Intel’s forecast, following strong data-centre growth.
- [Go’s new JSON packages](https://go.dev/blog/jsonv2): Go’s JSON redesign separates syntax from semantics, tightens default validation and adds streaming customization while preserving compatibility with the original package.
- [Linux on an RTX Spark laptop](https://www.mindstudio.ai/blog/linux-nvidia-rtx-spark-laptop): MindStudio reports an MSI laptop’s native Linux inference slowed sharply on mains power, tracing the gap to missing platform power-management support; Windows and WSL retained GPU boost.
- [The Internals of PostgreSQL](https://www.interdb.jp/pg/index.html): Hironobu Suzuki’s reference maps PostgreSQL’s cooperating subsystems, from concurrency control and buffering to recovery and replication.
- [Guerrilla bike lanes](https://99percentinvisible.org/article/guerrilla-bike-lanes-san-francisco-makes-illicit-infrastructure-permanent/): Kurt Kohlstedt’s 2016 account describes San Francisco retaining activists’ temporary lane barriers while arranging an official installation.
- [Ripple-65B Preview](https://www.ricursive.com/ripple): Ricursive releases a dense coding model and reports a TerminalBench 2.1 score of 80.5, with $32 million in total development and training costs.
- [Gemini’s activity controls](https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/16836988): Google says Gemini activity can train its models when Keep Activity is enabled, including summaries and excerpts from connected apps.
- [Provable inference](https://www.anthropic.com/research/provable-inference): Anthropic’s FlashProve prototype verifies outputs against committed model weights; its reported proof generation still costs roughly 70–95 times ordinary inference compute.
- [Pierre acquisition](https://openai.com/index/openai-to-acquire-pierre/): OpenAI plans to acquire Pierre, whose file-tree and diff libraries already power parts of Codex, while retaining its open-source projects.
- [Mundane Halloween](https://spoon-tamago.com/japan-jimi-mundane-halloween-costumes-2026/): Spoon & Tamago collects Japanese costumes built around small, recognizable social moments, including wrestling with a designer tap.
- [Conductor joins Meta](https://www.conductor.build/announcement): Conductor says its cloud service becomes read-only on November 13 and closes on November 20, with exports available before deletion begins.
- [Binance’s breach investigation](https://www.theregister.com/security/2026/11/03/north-korean-fake-job-interview-led-to-63b-binance-heist/5307398): Jessica Lyons reports Binance attributes its $6.3 billion theft to a multi-stage compromise that began with recruitment-themed malware and reached wallet deployment credentials.
- [Verification and knowledge](https://blog.cosmos-institute.org/p/verification-is-not-knowledge): Jessica Hullman argues that scientific knowledge needs people who can explain, challenge and repair results, beyond checking whether an answer passes a procedure.
- [GitHub’s November 4 incident](https://www.githubstatus.com/incidents/smtdswd1h2w4): GitHub reports restored service after errors affected Git operations, pull requests, issues, API requests and Actions.
- [Active faults beneath Valles Marineris](https://lamont.columbia.edu/news/giant-canyon-mars-still-tectonically-active-today): Researchers interpret InSight seismic waves as a deep strike-slip marsquake, suggesting ancient faults still rupture on Mars.
- [Kitty 0.50](https://sw.kovidgoyal.net/kitty/changelog/): Kitty adds docked terminal windows and session restoration after logout, alongside fixes for remote-control authentication and memory errors.
- [A posttraining data commons](https://openathena.ai/blog/there-is-no-posttraining-data-commons/): David Hall argues that useful reinforcement-learning tasks require manufactured environments, intent and graders that ordinary document collections do not preserve.
- [Kino’s shared editing timeline](https://kino.ai/): Luke Igel describes a code-based video editor that keeps footage, timeline edits and review notes addressable for collaborators and agents.
